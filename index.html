Security Awareness

Keep Your Wallet Information Private

Simple habits can help reduce the risk of scams, impersonation, and accidental disclosure of sensitive wallet information.

🛡

Protect Your Wallet

Your recovery information should remain private.

  • ✓Never share your 12/24-word recovery phrase or private key.
  • ✓Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • ✓Do install remote-access software at another person's request.
  • ✓Do not send cryptocurrency to unlock, verify, or recover a wallet.
  • ✓Use official wallet documentation and verified channels for account-specific support.
  • ✓Be cautious of fake support accounts and suspicious links.
